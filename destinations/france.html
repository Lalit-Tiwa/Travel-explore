France

France is a beautiful country known for its rich history, romantic cities, famous landmarks, art, fashion and delicious cuisine.

About France

France is a country in Western Europe and is one of the world's most popular travel destinations. It is famous for its beautiful architecture, museums, countryside, food and cultural heritage.

Top Places to Visit

  • Eiffel Tower
  • Louvre Museum
  • Palace of Versailles
  • French Riviera
  • Mont Saint-Michel

Best Time to Visit

Spring and autumn are great times to visit France because the weather is comfortable and the tourist crowds are generally smaller.
