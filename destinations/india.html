India

India is a diverse and colorful country known for its ancient history, beautiful landscapes, rich culture, delicious food and incredible monuments.

About India

India is a country in South Asia with a rich cultural heritage and thousands of years of history. From mountains and beaches to historic cities and temples, India offers a wide variety of travel experiences.

Top Places to Visit

  • Taj Mahal, Agra
  • Jaipur, Rajasthan
  • Goa
  • Kerala
  • Manali

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit India depends on the region. In general, October to March is a popular period for exploring many parts of the country.
