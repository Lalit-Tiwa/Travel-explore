London, England

London is one of the world's most famous cities, known for its history, iconic landmarks, culture and beautiful architecture.

About London

London is the capital city of England and the United Kingdom. It is famous for its royal history, museums, historic buildings, shopping areas and vibrant culture.

Top Places to Visit

  • Big Ben
  • London Eye
  • Buckingham Palace
  • Tower Bridge
  • British Museum

Best Time to Visit

Spring and summer are popular times to visit London because the weather is generally pleasant and many outdoor attractions can be enjoyed.
