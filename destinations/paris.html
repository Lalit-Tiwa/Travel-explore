Paris, France

Paris is one of the most beautiful and popular destinations in the world.

About Paris

Paris is the capital city of France and is famous for its beautiful architecture, art, culture and food. The city is also known as the City of Light.

Top Places to Visit

  • Eiffel Tower
  • Louvre Museum
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral
  • Arc de Triomphe
  • Champs-Élysées

Best Time to Visit

Spring and autumn are great times to visit Paris because the weather is generally comfortable.
